---

**Designers, content creators, and non-artists who need visuals quickly** (B2C — individual users making a personal purchase decision).

### 2. Why do they need it?
Creating custom images is slow and expensive; AI cuts this to seconds

  > What the product says: _"ImageToPrompts turns images into detailed, editable prompts for AI image generators"_

### 3. How does it find users?
**AI tool directories** (There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, Toolify) drive high-intent passive traffic. Product Hunt launch for initial spike. Before/after demos on Twitter/X — the output is inherently shareable.

### 5. What can I learn from this?
- AI wrapper products succeed on UX, not model quality — the underlying AI is a commodity. The product is the workflow, the UI, and the presets.

_Image To Prompts helps designers, content creators, and non-artists who need visuals quickly by letting them imagetoprompts turns images into detailed, editable prompts for ai image generators._
